What to Expect

Looking for a Calgary bridal hairstylist for your wedding day? I offer professional on-location bridal hair services in Calgary for brides and wedding parties, creating a relaxed, seamless, and luxury getting-ready experience from start to finish. On your wedding morning, I’ll travel directly to your home, hotel, or venue fully equipped with professional hairstyling tools, products, and supplies. I’m also available for destination weddings, bringing a calm and personalized bridal hair experience wherever your celebration takes place. Before the big day, you can send inspiration photos or book a bridal hair trial to finalize your wedding hairstyle, veil placement, and styling details. Together, we’ll confirm timelines and scheduling to ensure your wedding morning runs smoothly and you’re ready for every commitment throughout the day.

Every bridal hairstyle is designed to feel polished, secure, and long lasting through your ceremony, photos, and reception.

Event Styling

In addition to bridal hairstyling, I also offer event hair styling for special occasions, photoshoots, content creation, galas, parties, and professional events. Whether you’re attending an important celebration or stepping in front of a camera, I’ll create a polished hairstyle that feels elevated, effortless, and tailored to you.

Every look is designed to last throughout your event while helping you feel confident and camera ready. If you need to look great in front of a camera or crowd, I’m your girl.

Film Industry Experience

As a member of the Alberta film industry, I’ve had the opportunity to work on productions filming throughout the province, collaborating on projects for both television and streaming platforms. Through this experience, I’ve worked on productions including The Last of Us, The Abandons, and more, creating polished, camera ready hairstyling for on-screen talent and media productions.

About Me

Hi, I’m Stacy!

I’m your bridal hairstylist specializing in wedding hair for brides and bridal parties in Calgary and surrounding areas. I believe your wedding morning should feel just as beautiful as it looks, which is why I create a calm, personal, and seamless experience from our first conversation to the final touch. From soft romantic waves to elegant updos, I design timeless, photo-ready bridal hairstyles that feel like you — elevated, effortless, and made to last through every moment of your wedding day.
